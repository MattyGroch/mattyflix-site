Getting the most out of Mattyflix

A few Plex settings make a big difference to picture, sound and subtitles. Most take a minute to change, and you only need to do it once per device.

Quick checklist Picture quality Audio and subtitles Surround sound Nice extras When something's wrong

Quick checklist

  • Streaming quality set to Original or Maximum, with automatic adjusting turned off.
  • Your audio and subtitle languages set once on your Plex account, so every device picks the right tracks.
  • Your TV or streaming box on a wired connection if you watch in 4K.
  • Surround sound passthrough turned on if you have a soundbar or receiver.
  • Picking a text subtitle track (SRT) when there's a choice. It's easier on the server.

Picture quality

Out of the box, Plex often plays things at a lower quality than you'd expect, especially away from home. Setting quality to Original (or Maximum) tells Plex to send the file exactly as it is, with no loss in picture. It's also easier on our server, since nothing has to be converted on the fly.

Every app labels these settings a little differently, so look for the closest match on your device.

Smart TVs, Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV

Open Settings (or your profile picture → Settings), then Video Quality:

  • Adjust Automatically (or Auto): Off
  • Home Streaming: Maximum or Original
  • Remote Streaming: Maximum or Original. Mattyflix always counts as remote for you, since it isn't in your home, so this is the one that matters most.

Roku

Select your profile name (top left) → Settings → Videos:

  • Remote Quality and Local Quality: Original
  • Direct Play: Auto or Enabled
  • Allow Direct Stream: Enabled

Apple TV, iPhone, iPad and Android phones

Open your profile → Settings → Quality (sometimes under Video):

  • Remote Streaming: Original or Maximum
  • On phones, you might set a lower quality for Cellular to save your data plan, and keep Wi-Fi at Original.

In a web browser (app.plex.tv)

Click the Settings wrench (top right) → Quality under Plex Web, then Save Changes:

  • Automatically Adjust Quality: Off
  • Remote Streaming / Video Quality: Maximum
  • Play smaller videos at original quality: On
Buffering after changing this? Original needs a fast, steady connection, especially for 4K. Drop the quality a step (for example to 20 Mbps 1080p or 8 Mbps 1080p), or turn automatic adjusting back on. You can also change quality for just the current video: during playback, open the settings cog and pick a different quality.

Some movies have more than one version, like 4K and 1080p. Plex picks the best one for your setup, but you can choose with More (···) → Play Version on the movie's page.

Audio and subtitles

Your language preferences live on your Plex account, not on each device, so you only set them once. The easiest place is a web browser: sign in at app.plex.tv → Settings → Account, and look for the Languages or Audio & Subtitles section.

  • Automatically select audio and subtitle tracks: On
  • Preferred audio language: your language, for example English. This stops movies starting in a dubbed or commentary track.
  • Auto-select subtitle mode: pick what suits you:
    • Shown with foreign audio: subtitles appear automatically when the audio isn't in your language. Great for anime and foreign films.
    • Always enabled: subtitles on for everything.
    • Manually selected: subtitles only when you turn them on.
  • Preferred subtitle language: your language.
Subtitles causing buffering? Subtitles copied from Blu-rays (listed as PGS) are images, and many devices can't draw them, so our server has to bake them into the video as it plays. If there's an SRT track in the same language, pick that one instead.

You can always switch tracks for the current video from the player's audio and subtitle menu. Plex remembers your choice for that show.

Surround sound

Many movies on Mattyflix have 5.1 or 7.1 surround audio, including Dolby and DTS. If you have a soundbar or receiver, let it handle that audio directly:

  • In the Plex app on your TV or streaming box, open Settings → Audio and turn Passthrough (or Audio passthrough) on, or set it to the formats your equipment supports.
  • If you get silence or crackling afterwards, your setup doesn't support that format. Set passthrough back to Off or Auto.

No soundbar? Leave passthrough off, and Plex will mix the audio down for your TV speakers.

Nice extras

  • Skip intros and credits. Watch for the Skip Intro and Skip Credits buttons. Some apps can skip them automatically under Settings → Experience or Playback.
  • Auto-play the next episode. Look for Auto Play in the app's playback settings.
  • No trailers before movies. If movies start with trailers, turn off Cinema Trailers in the app's settings.
  • Keep Mattyflix front and center. On the Home screen, Trending on Mattyflix shows what everyone's watching this week. You can pin Mattyflix's libraries to your sidebar from the library list.
  • Request anything that's missing on Mattyflix Search, or turn on Watchlist auto-requests there so anything you add to your Plex Watchlist gets requested for you.

When something's wrong

  1. Buffering or stuttering: lower the quality for that video, switch to an SRT subtitle track (or turn subtitles off to test), and use a wired connection for 4K if you can.
  2. Nothing will play, or Mattyflix is missing: make sure you're signed in to the Plex account you accepted your invite with. Then check the server dashboard or the Discord to see whether something's going on.
  3. Wrong language or no subtitles: pick the track from the player's menu, and set your account defaults as described above.
  4. "Too many streams" or a stream limit message: each account can play up to 3 streams at once. Stop one on another device and try again.
  5. Still stuck? Ask in the Discord. Tell us what you were watching, on which device, and roughly when, so we can look it up.
