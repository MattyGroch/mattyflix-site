Help and tips

Getting the best picture

  • Set streaming quality to Original (or Maximum) in the Plex app's settings.
  • Buffering? Lower the quality for that stream. It's the quickest fix.
  • For 4K on a TV, a wired connection beats Wi-Fi.
  • Turn off cinema trailers so movies start right away.

Common fixes

  • Can't see Mattyflix? Make sure you're signed in to the same Plex account you accepted the invite with.
  • Wrong language or no subtitles? Pick the audio or subtitle track from the player's settings.
  • Still stuck? Ask in the Discord, or report it on Mattyflix Search.
