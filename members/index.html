Tips and quick fixes

Full guide ›
Picture looks soft or blurry
In the Plex app's settings, set streaming quality to Original (or Maximum).
Buffering
Lower the quality for that stream. For 4K on a TV, a wired connection beats Wi-Fi.
Can't find Mattyflix in Plex
Make sure you're signed in to the same Plex account you accepted your invite with.
Wrong language or no subtitles
Pick the audio or subtitle track from the player's settings.
Movies start with trailers
Turn off Cinema Trailers in the Plex app's settings.
Still stuck?
Ask in the Discord.
